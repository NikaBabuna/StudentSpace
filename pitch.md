# StudentSpace

## The problem

Tutoring is personal, but managing it is a mess.

Tutors juggle WhatsApp threads, spreadsheets, Google Drive folders, and bank transfers just to run a handful of classes. Parents have no visibility into what their child is actually doing. Students lose track of deadlines. Employers and agencies have no way to oversee the tutors they pay without being physically present.

There's no single place where all of this lives together.

---

## What StudentSpace is

StudentSpace is a structured workspace for private tutoring — built for tutors, students, parents, and the organisations behind them.

Every class gets its own space. Inside it, a tutor can schedule lessons, post homework, upload materials, run a live chat, and track payment cycles — all in one place. Students submit work and receive feedback. Parents observe progress. Employers get oversight across every class their tutors run.

It's not a marketplace. It's not a video call tool. It's the operational layer that sits underneath a tutoring relationship and makes it run properly.

---

## Who it's for

**Tutors** — independent teachers who manage multiple students and want a professional, organised way to do it without stitching together five different apps.

**Students** — who want a clear view of their upcoming lessons, active homework, deadlines, and feedback in one place.

**Parents** — who want visibility into their child's progress without having to ask the tutor for updates every week.

**Employers and agencies** — organisations that employ or contract tutors and need oversight across classes, payment cycles, and performance without interfering in the teaching itself.

---

## What it does

- **Schedule** — weekly calendar with lesson tracking, makeup scheduling, and attendance records
- **Homework** — post assignments with attachments, collect submissions, give written feedback
- **Materials** — organised file groups with drag-and-drop upload
- **Chat** — live class chat with Supabase Realtime
- **Payment cycles** — automatic cycle tracking based on completed hours, with per-cycle payment amounts in any currency
- **Analytics** — attendance rates, missed lesson percentages, homework completion, earnings breakdown — all filterable by time range
- **Parent linking** — parents can link to their child's account and see their progress across shared classes
- **Employer dashboard** — organisations see all tutors and students across their classes, with a dedicated interface separate from the standard user experience
